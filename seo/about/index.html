About InfiNet Spectre

Spectre is an AI dark-web intelligence tool built by the InfiNet team to give authorized researchers a faster, safer way to investigate the Tor ecosystem.

What We're Building

Most dark-web research today is still manual: copy-paste queries into a Tor search engine, read through pages of forum threads, and try to remember what you've already seen. Spectre replaces that loop with an AI-driven search-and-summarize workflow so a 4-hour triage becomes a 20-minute brief.

Why It Matters

Threat-intel teams, incident responders, journalists, and fraud analysts all need the same basic capability — quickly find out whether something specific is being discussed, sold, or leaked on the dark web. That capability today is either expensive enterprise software or artisanal tooling built in-house. Spectre exists to change that.

Authorized Research Only

Spectre is built, marketed, and operated as a tool for legitimate, authorized research. We actively suspend accounts that attempt to misuse the platform and cooperate with law enforcement where legally required. If you are not sure whether your intended use is appropriate, email us first.

Part of InfiNet

InfiNet Spectre is one of several products from InfiNet — our umbrella brand covering cybersecurity audits, AI automation, and custom web and mobile development. We also operate InfiNet AI, an uncensored fine-tuned model platform.

Contact

The fastest way to reach us is through the InfiNet contact page: infinet.services/contact/. We read every message and respond to legitimate research and partnership inquiries.
